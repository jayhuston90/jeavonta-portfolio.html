About Me

I wasn’t born with a keyboard in my hands, but somewhere along the way, coding became my second language. What started as a curiosity turned into a passion—and eventually, a career. I taught myself the fundamentals late at night, long after putting my kids to bed, driven by the belief that I could build a better life for my family. Balancing code commits with parent-teacher conferences isn’t easy, but it’s worth every second. Being a husband and father is at the core of who I am. It’s what fuels me to keep pushing forward, even when deadlines loom and sleep is a luxury. Each website I build, every app I deploy, carries a little piece of that dedication. I’m not just writing lines of code—I’m building a future. One where my kids can see what hard work, perseverance, and love for what you do can really create.
